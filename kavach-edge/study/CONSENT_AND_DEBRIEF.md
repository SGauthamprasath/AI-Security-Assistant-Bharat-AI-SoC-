# Consent and debrief

## Consent (also shown on the first screen of the app)
You are invited to try a prototype app that warns about scam messages and suspicious network activity. It takes about 12 minutes. You will read
10 fictional messages and answer questions about them. We record only your answers, timings and a participant number - no name, phone number
or personal messages. Participation is voluntary; you can stop at any time without giving a reason. The messages are fictional; do not tap any link.
By ticking the box you confirm you are 18 or older and agree to take part.

## Debrief (say aloud, 1 minute)
"Thank you. Some of those messages were real-looking scams we wrote, and some were genuine. We compared two ways of showing the app's result: a technical
view and a plain-language view. Neither is a test of you. In real life: never share an OTP, PIN or CVV; do not tap links in unexpected messages; verify using the
official app or the number on your card; and report cyber-fraud at 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. Any questions? What was the most confusing part?"
